BlowTorch 2 v2.5.0
Bluetooth keyboard support. A key can send a line, and the number pad can walk. .scrollnew jumps to the newest text.
A chat reply keeps the conversation's name. The world's echo replaces your bubble. Back returns to the list. ANSI colors are per conversation.
Up and Down walk commands you have sent. .prefix on: ki then Up finds kill goblin.
A plugin can rewrite a line, a send, or one GMCP message, and can fetch a page beside the game.
